Number Slide Pro

First Google Play upload · Android · com.bilvok.numberslidepro

1. Prepare the upload bundle

Extract the entire ZIP. On Windows, run Start-First-Upload.cmd with JDK 17+ or Android Studio installed. On the first run, enter a new keystore password at the local Java prompts. Save the password and back up the encrypted upload.p12 file at the location shown in the terminal. The helper reuses that key on subsequent runs. If you already have an upload key, launch the command with your existing keystore path and alias as arguments.

After success, upload NumberSlidePro-upload-signed.aab. Keep the private keystore on your device. The APK in android/ is available for direct installation.

2. Create the app and add the listing

In your Play Console, select Create app. Use Number Slide Pro, Game, and English (United States). Confirm the price, countries, and public support email for your account.

  • Short description and full description
  • 512 × 512 icon and 1024 × 500 feature graphic
  • Five phone screenshots, already numbered in display order
  • Privacy policy URL: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/subair71/number-slide-pro/main/assets/privacy_policy.txt

3. Submit for review

Complete the app access, ads, target audience, content rating and Data safety declarations for your build. The game uses anonymous Firebase authentication and online leaderboard records. Check your release’s AdMob settings when answering the ads declaration. Add the signed AAB to the track offered for your account and use the release notes. Review Google’s checks and send the changes for review.

If the account requires closed testing, complete the test and apply for production access in the dashboard. Only Play Console can confirm submission, review or availability.
