Slide numbered tiles into the open space until every number is in order. Start with a 3×3 board, take on the classic 4×4, or try to untangle all 24 tiles on a 5×5.

Play a fresh shuffle or return for the daily challenge. Each move counts toward your score. When the last tile clicks into place, see your time, move count and rewards. Practice the same starting board to find a shorter path.

Earn coins, gems and XP through play. In the Theme Vault, try tile skins, board frames and movement effects on a preview board before you equip them. Your collection stays saved on your device.

The online leaderboard compares personal bests by board size. Play under a generated player name, track your own progress, or share a finished run using the Android share menu.
